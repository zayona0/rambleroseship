FROM THE DECK OF THE RAMBLEROSE

To the outside world, and to every distant corner of the sprawling Seven Kingdoms:

From the depths of the darkness that precedes the dawn, and aboard the "Ramblerose"—a ship that neither compromises with the wind nor fears the waves—we send you our message. Not as decrees of a tyrant ruler, nor as the orders of a legislator closing doors, but as the sigh of seagulls crossing the seas, witnessing what sleepy eyes behind walls fail to see.

— Remain free, even if it costs you all the waves of the sea.
